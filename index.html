Professional focus

Building useful systems around people, decisions, and change.

I approach management as a craft of clarity: setting direction, making trade-offs explicit, and helping people work with better information. My current interests sit where finance discipline meets transformation, AI-enabled workflows, and regulatory understanding.

  • Finance and business judgment Connecting numbers, risks, and operational realities into decisions people can act on.
  • Transformation and ways of working Improving processes, documentation, and handovers so teams can move with less friction.
  • Technology and AI fluency Exploring practical automation and AI workflows that support professionals rather than distract them.
  • Legal and regulatory awareness Keeping structure, traceability, and accountability in view when work touches rules and obligations.
